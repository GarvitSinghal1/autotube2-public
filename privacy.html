Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 24, 2026

AutoTube ("we", "our", or "us") operates a personal automated tool that discovers public datasets, renders animated data visualizations, and posts them to your authorized accounts (YouTube and TikTok).

1. Information We Collect

We do not collect, store, or share any personal information or usage data. The platform runs entirely as a private automation script. Any tokens or credentials provided during the authorization process are stored securely in your private environment variables (e.g. GitHub Secrets or local .env file) and are used solely to upload videos directly to your channels.

2. API Permissions

By authorizing the application on YouTube or TikTok, you grant it the permission to upload and publish videos on your behalf. These permissions are used exclusively to post the generated data visualization videos. We do not access, read, or modify any other account data or personal messages.

3. Third-Party Services

We use the following third-party APIs to run the automation pipeline:

  • Google Gemini API: Used to select topics and generate video metadata.
  • YouTube Data API: Used to upload videos to your YouTube channel.
  • TikTok Content Posting API: Used to upload videos to your TikTok profile.
Please refer to their respective privacy policies for how they handle data.

4. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Any updates will be pushed directly to this hosted document.
